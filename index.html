03Who is this course for?

Adults interested in longevity science and using health data to inform daily habits. No medical or science background is needed. Classes are live on Zoom, in English, in groups of 8 to 15, and every class is recorded.

04What happens in a typical session?

50 minutes live with a certified longevity instructor. A short teaching block on the week's pillar, then work on your own data and questions, then one change to make before the next class.

05What do I finish with?

A written personal protocol covering nutrition, movement, sleep, stress and supplements. The course includes a 14-day Lingo biosensor and guidance on understanding health data.

06Is this medical advice?

No. The program is educational and focused on helping you understand the science of longevity and your own health data. We do not diagnose, treat, or provide clinical care. For medical concerns, keep working with your physician.

07What if it is not for me?

Your enrollment is fully refundable within 14 days of registration. After that, the enrollment agreement and applicable law determine refund eligibility. See our cancellation and refund policy.
